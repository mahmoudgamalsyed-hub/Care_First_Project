## 💡 Project Idea

**Care First** is a social media business page focused on medical equipment for home use. The page presents products intended to support people and families who need medical devices in a home-care setting.

Our project will review the page's current digital presence and build a structured marketing approach around its products, audience, content, and communication.

The aim is not simply to publish more posts. It is to make the page more informative, credible, consistent, and customer-focused.

### 💎 Our Value Direction
- **Clarity:** Explain what each product is and what it is designed for.
- **Trust:** Use accurate, transparent, and responsible product information.
- **Accessibility:** Make product information and enquiry steps easy to understand.
- **Consistency:** Build a recognizable visual and communication style.
- **Engagement:** Encourage relevant questions and meaningful interaction.

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

1. Assess the current social media presence and identify improvement opportunities.
2. Understand the target audience and its needs when considering home medical equipment.

4. Establish clear content pillars and a practical publishing plan.
5. Improve the quality and consistency of the page's visual identity.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

*KPIs will be used to evaluate progress. The team must record a baseline before setting final numerical targets.*

| KPI | What It Measures | How to Track |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reach** | Number of unique accounts reached | Instagram Insights |
| **Impressions / Views** | Total content exposures or views | Instagram Insights |
| **Engagement Rate** | Audience interaction relative to reach or followers | Engagement ÷ selected base × 100 |
| **Follower Growth** | Change in follower count | (New followers − lost followers) ÷ starting followers × 100 |
| **Profile Visits** | Interest in the business profile | Instagram Insights |
| **Link / Contact Clicks** | Actions taken to contact or learn more | Profile and platform insights |
| **Enquiries** | Relevant product questions received | Manually logged messages and calls |
| **Content Consistency** | Delivery against the publishing plan | Published posts ÷ planned posts × 100 |
| **Cost per Result** | Advertising cost for each defined campaign result | Ad spend ÷ results |

### 📋 KPI Tracking Rules
- Record the starting baseline before implementation.
- Review organic performance weekly.
- Review paid campaign performance according to campaign duration and spend.
- Compare results with the previous reporting period.
- Interpret metrics in context; follower growth alone does not prove business success.
- Do not report a target as an achieved result.

## ✍️ Content & Communication Guidelines

*Because Care First operates in the medical equipment category, accuracy and trust are essential.*

- Verify product specifications and claims before publishing.
- Use clear Arabic wording that customers can understand.
- Avoid unsupported treatment, prevention, or guaranteed-result claims.
- Do not provide individual diagnosis or medical advice through marketing content.
- Refer medical-use questions to an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
- Use clear product images and disclose relevant product details.
- Keep captions, designs, and calls to action consistent with the brand.
